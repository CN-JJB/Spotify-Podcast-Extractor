# Spotify-Podcast-Extractor — Privacy

## Privacy policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026 · Applies to version 1.1.0

Spotify-Podcast-Extractor is an open-source project maintained by [CN-JJB on GitHub](https://github.com/CN-JJB/Spotify-Podcast-Extractor) . Its single purpose is to export an episode’s available Spotify transcript to a file you choose.

### Information processed on your device

After you click the extension, it reads the current episode’s title, podcast name and link, episode URL, transcript text, speaker labels, chapter labels and transcript notices. These are website content and the URL of the episode you selected. They are processed locally to create the preview and export. The extension also temporarily uses the source tab’s identifier and the status of downloads it starts.

### Storage and sharing

The transcript is held in the export tab’s memory while that tab is open. The extension does not store a transcript database, send content or URLs to the developer or any third-party server, or run analytics, ads or tracking. It does not sell data or transfer data for advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions. Language selection is represented in the local export tab’s URL; it is not uploaded.

Downloaded files remain in the location you choose. Your browser may retain its normal download and browsing records independently of this extension. You control those files and records using your operating system and browser. Closing the export tab releases the extension’s in-memory transcript; uninstalling the extension does not delete files you previously downloaded.

Help, source-code and support links only open when you click them. Visiting GitHub or Spotify is subject to that website’s own policies; the extension does not attach transcript content to these links.

### Permissions
- **activeTab:** temporary access to the tab where you invoked the extension.
- **scripting:** read that tab’s transcript panel and check loading stability.
- **downloads:** create the requested export and monitor that download. The extension does not scan your download history.

The extension does not read authentication cookies, account credentials, payment information, your Obsidian vault or unrelated browsing history. It does not bypass website access controls.

### Limited use and contact

Use of information received through Chrome APIs is limited to this disclosed export feature and adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. For questions, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/CN-JJB/Spotify-Podcast-Extractor/issues)  or the developer contact in the Chrome Web Store listing. GitHub Issues are public; do not include private information. If you separately contact the developer, you choose what to send through that channel; the extension does not attach transcripts or logs automatically.

Any change in these practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where relevant, in the extension and store listing.

## 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日 · 适用于 1.1.0 版本

本扩展由 [GitHub 用户 CN-JJB](https://github.com/CN-JJB/Spotify-Podcast-Extractor)  维护，唯一用途是将用户所选 Spotify 单集已有的转录导出为文件。

### 在本机处理哪些信息

仅在你点击扩展后，读取当前单集标题、节目名和链接、单集网址、转录正文、说话人、章节及转录提示。这些属于网页内容及所选单集网址，仅用于在本机生成预览和导出文件。扩展还临时使用原标签页编号，以及自己发起的下载状态。

### 保存与共享

转录保留在导出标签页的内存中。扩展不建立文字稿数据库，不将内容或网址发送给开发者或第三方，不包含统计上报、广告或追踪，不出售数据，也不为广告、信用或借贷决策转移数据。语言选择只体现在本地导出标签页网址中，不上传。

下载文件保存在你选择的位置。浏览器会独立管理其正常的下载记录和浏览记录，你可以通过浏览器或操作系统管理这些记录和文件。关闭导出页释放扩展内存中的文字稿；卸载扩展不会删除已下载文件。

源码、支持和网站链接仅在你点击时打开。访问 GitHub 或 Spotify 适用其自身政策，扩展不会向这些链接附带转录正文。

### 权限用途
- **activeTab：**在你主动调用扩展时，临时访问当前页面。
- **scripting：**读取该页面的转录面板并检查加载是否稳定。
- **downloads：**保存请求的导出文件，并监测该次下载；不扫描下载历史。

扩展不读取登录 Cookie、账号密码、支付信息、Obsidian 仓库或无关浏览历史，不绕过网站访问权限。

### 用途限制与联系

通过 Chrome API 获得的信息仅用于已披露的导出功能，并遵守 Chrome 网上应用店用户数据政策及 Limited Use 要求。问题可通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/CN-JJB/Spotify-Podcast-Extractor/issues)  或商店所列开发者联系方式反馈。GitHub Issues 公开可见，请勿提交私人信息。你另外联系开发者时，可自行决定发送哪些信息；扩展不会自动附带转录或日志。

若处理方式改变，会同步更新本政策，并按需要更新扩展及商店说明。
